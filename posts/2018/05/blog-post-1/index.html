During my PhD, I spent a lot of time searching, collecting, and preparing datasets for multitask learning. To help new researchers in the field, I am sharing the datasets that I collected over the years.

All the Global Climate Models combination datasets were created by our research group at University of Minnesota Twin Cities. For more context, see this paper.
