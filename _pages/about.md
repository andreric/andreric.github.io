<section class="home-hero">
  <p class="home-eyebrow">Machine Learning Research Scientist</p>
  <h1>Building machine learning systems for protein design and large-scale clinical data.</h1>
  <p class="home-lead">
    I am a Machine Learning Research Scientist in the <a href="https://www.llnl.gov">Machine Learning Group at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory</a>.
    My work focuses on scalable machine learning for noisy, real-world biological and clinical data, spanning protein design,
    developability modeling, and foundation models trained on national-scale healthcare records.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Protein Design</h2>
  <p>
    I work on antibody design, including library design for developability assays, machine learning models for antibody developability prediction,
    and computational design guided by protein folding tools. I am also interested in multi-substrate enzyme optimization and the de novo design of
    adhesive proteins for biomaterial engineering.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Clinical Machine Learning</h2>
  <p>
    I train foundation models from scratch on structured sequences of clinical events, without clinical notes, at large compute scale and fine-tune
    them for biosurveillance applications including syndromic surveillance and infectious disease detection. I also work on EHR-based diagnosis
    prediction, drug repurposing, and Bayesian multitask models for microbiome analysis.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Broader Research Interests</h2>
  <p>
    My broader interests include multitask learning, non-convex and combinatorial optimization, efficient pretraining of domain-specific foundation
    models, and uncertainty quantification.
  </p>
</section>
